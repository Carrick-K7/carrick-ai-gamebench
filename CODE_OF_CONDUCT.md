# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in GameBench a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique benchmark claims, code, data, and methodology rather than people.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when submitting tasks or model results.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, private trajectories,
  reviewer identities, or embargoed vulnerabilities.
- Accept responsibility, apologize to those affected by mistakes, and learn
  from the experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
misrepresentation of results, and publication of private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[private vulnerability reporting form](https://github.com/Carrick-K7/carrick-ai-gamebench/security/advisories/new).
Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy where
possible, and may remove content or restrict participation when necessary.

This policy is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
